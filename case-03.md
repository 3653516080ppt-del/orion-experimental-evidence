# CASE-03: Moving Failure Beyond the Planner

This six-case certification asked a narrow question: could the Planner produce an accepted implementation plan without repeating the planning failures found in earlier work?

It could. All 6 plans were returned in the required structured form and accepted, no runs were invalid, and none of the previously tracked Planner failures recurred. Every case then advanced to BUILD.

## Why It Matters

The tasks did not suddenly become successful. Instead, they stopped failing during planning and began failing later in the workflow. That distinction matters because it identifies where the next bottleneck actually sits.

## The Limit of the Evidence

Reaching BUILD is not the same as completing a task. This case does not certify Builder performance, prove universal Planner correctness, or establish end-to-end success.

Evidence status: The numbers were recalculated from preserved internal records rather than copied from the original summary. No external party has yet reproduced this case.
